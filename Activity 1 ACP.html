Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro (born February 5, 1985) is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward and captains the Saudi Pro League club Al-Nassr. Widely recognized as one of the greatest football players in history, his career is defined by record-breaking scoring feats, numerous major trophies, and global athletic dominance.

Early Life and Background Birth & Origins:

Born in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal, into an impoverished Catholic family.

Childhood Hardships:

Raised in a small home alongside his siblings (older brother Hugo and sisters Elma and Liliana Catia); his father, Jose Dinis Aveiro, struggled with alcoholism, while his mother, Maria Dolores dos Santos Aveiro, worked as a cook and cleaner.

 Early Football Passion:

Played street football with balls made of rags and socks, joining his first local team, Andorinha, at age seven.
